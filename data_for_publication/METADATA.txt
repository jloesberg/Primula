README for data

__CGOP_climatevalues_scaled.csv__

year: year of collected data

remaining columns are seasonal climate variables for fall (September-November), winter (December-February), spring (March-May), and summer (June-August)

climate variables are tot.precip (total precipitation), mean.max.temp (maximum temperature), mean.min.temp (minimum temperature), and mean.temp (mean temperature) for 2013-2023. Values have been scaled to a mean of 0 and standard deviation of 1

__Primula_Clipping_Experiment.csv__

year: year that data were collected

plot: plot number of experiment

tag: unique identifier for each individual plant

trt: rainfall experiment treatment

clip.treatment: treatment for clipping experiment (all flowers clipped, half flowers clipped, no flowers clipped)

no.clipped: number of flowers clipped in experiment

ht: height of flowering stalk from base of ground to top

rosetteL: longest length of rosette

rosetteW: width of rosette perpendicular to length measurement

ros.area: area of rosette calculated as the area of an ellipse (for current in year)

ros.areaT1: area of rosette calculated as the area of an ellipse (for subsequent year)

log.ros.area: natural logarithm transformation of ros.area

log.ros.areaT1: natural logarithm transformation of ros.areaT1

no.flowers: number of flowers made by plant at start of experiment

no.flowersT1: number of flowers made by plant in the following year

total.seeds: number of seeds made by plant

log.total.seeds:  natural logarithm transformation of total.seeds

no.success.cap: number of capsules that made seeds

pseeds: probability that plant made seeds (0 = did not make seeds, 1 = made seeds)

total.seedsT1: number of seeds made by plant in the following year

pflowerT1: probability of plant flowering the following year (0 = not flowering, 1 = flowering)

psurvivalT1: probability of plant being alive the following year (0 = dead, 1 = alive)

__Primula_Demography_Data_2016to2023.csv__

year: year that data were collected

plot: plot number of experiment

tag: unique identifier for each individual plant

trt: rainfall experiment treatment

clipping_experiment: whether plant was in the clipping experiment (1 = yes, NA = no)

rosetteL: longest length of rosette

rosetteW: width of rosette perpendicular to length measurement

psurvival: plant survival this year (0 = dead, 1 = alive)

psurvivalT1: probability of plant being alive the following year (0 = dead, 1 = alive)

pflower: whether plant is flowering this year (0 = not flowering, 1 = flowering)

pflowerT1: probability of plant flowering the following year (0 = not flowering, 1 = flowering)

ros.area: area of rosette calculated as the area of an ellipse (for current in year)

ros.areaT1: area of rosette calculated as the area of an ellipse (for subsequent year)

log.ros.area: natural logarithm transformation of ros.area

log.ros.areaT1: natural logarithm transformation of ros.areaT1

flow.sum: total number of reproductive structures at time of survey (buds, flowers, and capsules)

flow.sumT1: total number of reproductive structures at the following survey the next year (buds, flowers, and capsules)

total.seeds: number of seeds made by plant

total.seedsT1: number of seeds made by plant in the following year

percapseeds: per capita number of seeds produced per flower (number of seeds total divided by number of flowers

life_st: life stage (V = vegetative, U = underground or dormant, F = flowering, dead = dead)
